## 2. The outreach (exact)

**To clinic and pharmacy networks (demand):**
> "Hey Jonathan, you're dispensing across 21 sites. I work with licensed drug wholesalers built for exactly that. One contract, every clinic on it. Would love to send one your way."

**Pharmacy follow-up:**
> "When your wholesaler says it's on backorder, what do you do?"

**To wholesalers (supply):**
> "Hey Michael, I'm working a set of independent pharmacies and clinic networks in Texas, a few dispensing across 10+ sites, around what their primary keeps putting on backorder. Before I send anyone anywhere, I wanted to understand what you carry and where you ship."

The industry term is **"accounts":** "This instantly makes you look like an insider … instead of saying just a pharmacy, you say it's a pharmacy account."

## 4. The close email (exact as read)

> "Michael, taking this over from the other thread so I can send you the live brief properly. [I have a clinic network in Houston] … Their primary wholesaler has had them on allocation since June. Current need is roughly 400 bottles a month across [these medications]. They require a supplier to be VAWD and NABP accredited, [DSCSA compliant with T3 data], and able to bill their 340B accounts separately from WAC. They're ready to speak within the next 24 hours if the fit is there.
>
> The way I structure this is $12,000 for six to nine qualified pharmacy opportunities over the next 60 to 90 days. This account would be the first one. If you can handle the requirements above and we move forward, I'll make the introduction immediately once payment is taken care of, and then I'll bring you the remaining opportunities over the engagement. If I bring you somebody that clearly does not fit, I replace them."

Stripe product: **"Texas Pharmacy Account Origination: 60–90 Day Engagement."** The description covers 6–9 qualified opportunities matched to product, geography, license and compliance; the first released on payment and fit confirmation, the rest over 60–90 days; and the replacement clause.

He avoided the words "marketing services," "lead generation" and "appointment setting," which make you look like a vendor.

## 6. Frankie comparison

- **The pharmacy follow-up** ("When your wholesaler says it's on backorder, what do you do?") is a Frankie-style situation question.
- **Moving from a one-off to an engagement** at the first sale matches Frankie's coffee date → first date → marriage.

## 7. Rules from this case

**Transferable:**

- Find the gap where the incumbent supplier fails (backorders, allocation), not where there's no supplier at all.
- Use Reddit or forums for the buyer's own pain wording.
- A buyer reply containing a full brief means close now, quoting their exact requirements.
- Sell a time-boxed engagement (6–9 opportunities over 60–90 days) once the first account is real.
- Name the payment product with origination language, not "lead gen."

**Specific to this market:** NDCs, VAWD/NABP, DSCSA, 340B. **Healthcare referral fees can be regulated:** check before using.

**Do not copy:**

- The wholesaler email's "I'm working a set of independent pharmacies and clinic networks" implied existing accounts at level 2. The wholesaler then **assumed "several active accounts,"** and Saad didn't correct it.
- Under our rules, a 6–9 opportunity engagement may only be sold if you can honestly deliver it. Say what's real: "This would be the first account. I'm actively building the rest of the pipeline in Texas."
