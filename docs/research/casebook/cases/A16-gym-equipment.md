Why this case matters: it's the clearest catalog of the **kinds of replies** a two-sided campaign produces, and Saad's draft reply to each.

## 1. What he knew before sending

- **The timing window:** "Don't find gyms, find gyms before they become gyms" ("coming soon" and presale pages). Once a gym opens, someone has already sold it equipment.
- **The economics:** median build-out about $195k, much of it equipment.
- **Honesty level at send:** level 2.

## 2. The outreach (exact)

**To dealers (supply):**
> "I have gyms opening and operating across the US (franchise clubs and independents) actively lining up equipment, service and financing right now."

**To gyms (demand):**
> "I have refurbished commercial equipment dealers and financing partners actively taking on new gyms right now, usually well under the new equipment quote. Would love to send one your way."

He called it "the most boring message you can send … and Orangetheory replied." No personalization tricks.

## 4. How he counts the money

- **Counted:** Crunch ($2,997 sourcing) plus dealers on the 60–90 day promise.
- **Not counted:** Orangetheory. "That gym is real, but not ready. We don't count money." It counts only once a dealer is confirmed approved, and then at a higher price.

**His FAQ on the model:**

- **The same gym goes to 2–3 dealers.** The gym asked for options: "a pizza with three slices, pay once."
- **A dealer pays for a 60–90 day promise of 3–6 opportunities,** not for one gym. If the gym buys elsewhere, "we keep sending that dealer more gyms until they get what they paid for."
- **"Our promise was never this gym will buy from you … these are real gyms really buying; if one clearly does not fit, replace it."**
- **Paid before delivery, not negotiable.** "The replacement promise is what makes both sides comfortable paying first."

## 6. Frankie comparison

- **"I'd rather understand what you're buying first so I don't send you random options"** is Frankie's benevolent dictator: "I'm happy to answer, but first let's make sure we're a fit."
- **Treating replies as questions, not objections,** matches Frankie's "objections happen at the plan."

## 7. Rules from this case

**Transferable:**

- Most replies are one of four questions: who are the buyers, what are they buying, show me the sellers, or what are the rules.
- When a buyer asks for your supplier list, ask what they're buying first.
- Rule-bound buyers (franchise approved-supplier rules): check before you claim.
- Count only matches that are ready, not replies.

**Specific to this market:** "coming soon" gym pages, franchise approved-supplier rules.

**Do not copy:**

- "I have gyms opening … actively lining up equipment" and "I have … dealers and financing partners actively taking on new gyms" were sent at level 2.
- "I work with commercial vendors that can supply at scale" (in his Orangetheory reply) holds only if a dealer like that has actually engaged. Here three dealers had replied, so it was arguably true at that point. That's the right standard.
