# What Have You Done for Me Lately? — Rep Scorecard

Goal: readers judge representatives by what they *did*, not by the letter next
to their name.

## Steps
1. **Get the current roster** from the Congress.gov members API (state = AL)
   and Open States (Alabama legislature). Never rely on a remembered list.
2. **Pull this week's activity** for each member: roll-call votes (House Clerk,
   Senate XML), bills sponsored or cosponsored, committee actions, and earmarks
   or grants announced for Alabama (USAspending, press releases).
3. **Pick the 2–4 actions that matter most** to Alabamians this week. Skip
   procedural noise unless it decided the outcome.
4. **Impact test** for each one: what the measure does, and who in Alabama
   gains or loses (jobs, healthcare, farm income, prices, schools, safety,
   rural services), with numbers and a source.
5. **Say vs. do**: when a member's public statement (press release, social
   post, town hall) conflicts with their vote, show both with links and dates,
   and quote them exactly.
6. **Give credit** where a member delivered for Alabama, from any party.

## Format
Per member featured: **Name (Party–District)**: what they did → what it means
for Alabama → [vote/bill link]. Close with a "Their contact" line (official
phone/site) and one question readers could ask them.

## Rules
- Facts and links only. Characterize votes by what the bill does, citing text
  or CBO, not by partisan talking points.
- Don't guess motives. Say "voted against X, which would have…", not "doesn't
  care about…".
- Same scrutiny for every party and every member.
