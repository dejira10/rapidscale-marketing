# Around the State and Opportunity Board

## Around the State (3–5 items)
- **Community spotlights**: local wins, people and organizations doing good
  work, small-town stories. Spread them across regions over time (North,
  Birmingham metro, Black Belt, Wiregrass, Gulf Coast, Montgomery, Tuscaloosa,
  etc.).
- **Events** in the coming two weeks: festivals, town halls, public hearings,
  sports, cultural events. Give date, place and link.
- Sources: Alabama Tourism calendar, city and county sites, local news,
  university calendars (see `alabama-newsletter/references/sources.md`).

## Opportunity Board (4–6 items)
- **Hiring news**: new plants and expansions from Alabama Commerce and
  "Made in Alabama" (company, county, job count, timeline).
- **Open roles and job fairs**: AlabamaWorks and JobLink. Favor roles that are
  in demand and pay well.
- **Training**: Alabama Community College System, apprenticeships, free
  certifications.
- **Grants and programs**: small-business grants (ADECA, SBA, grants.gov),
  scholarships, deadlines.

## Format
**Bold lead** — one or two sentences — where, when, how to apply — [link].
Rule: list only verified, current opportunities with a working link and a
deadline if one exists.
