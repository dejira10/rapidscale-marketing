# Alabama Policy Tracker

## Steps
1. **Federal**: new actions in the issue window from the Congress.gov API and
   Federal Register API (sources in `alabama-newsletter/references/sources.md`).
   Keep items with an Alabama angle: delegation votes or sponsorship, or
   effects on Alabama programs, industries, bases, farms, ports or budgets.
2. **State**: bill movement, votes, fiscal notes and executive orders from
   ALISON, LSA, Open States and the governor's office. If the legislature is
   out of session, cover interim committees, agency rules, special sessions and
   prefiled bills.
3. For each item, record: what it does (plain language, from the bill text or
   summary, not from headlines); its status; who voted how (hand off to
   `alabama-rep-scorecard`); who in Alabama is affected and how much (use CBO,
   fiscal notes, Census/KFF/USDA data); and what readers can do (comment
   periods, hearings, contacting an office).
4. Rank by number of Alabamians affected × size of the effect. Pick the top 3–5.

## Item format
**Bold headline-style lead.** 2–4 sentences on what happened.
**What it means for you:** one or two concrete sentences.
[Source](url)

Rule: if the effect isn't in a source, say "effect not yet estimated" instead
of guessing.
