# Alabama Newsletter — Sources

Prefer primary sources. Check each one is still live when you build the n8n
workflow, since URLs and APIs change.

## Federal policy and votes
- **Congress.gov API** (api.congress.gov, free key from api.data.gov): bills,
  actions, cosponsors, members. Filter members by state `AL` to get the
  *current* delegation. Never hardcode names.
- **House roll-call votes**: clerk.house.gov/Votes (XML per vote).
- **Senate roll-call votes**: senate.gov → Legislation & Records → Votes (XML).
- **Federal Register API** (federalregister.gov/api/v1): rules, proposed rules
  and executive orders. Search for terms tied to Alabama (agriculture, SNAP,
  Medicaid, military bases, NASA/Huntsville, ports, etc.).
- **GovTrack** (govtrack.us): readable vote summaries and member pages.
- **CBO** (cbo.gov): cost estimates and state-level effects.
- **USAspending.gov API**: federal money flowing into Alabama by county.

## State policy and votes
- **Alabama Legislature (ALISON)**: alison.legislature.state.al.us — bills,
  status, votes, calendars.
- **Alabama Legislative Services Agency**: lsa.state.al.us — fiscal notes and
  bill summaries.
- **Open States API** (v3.openstates.org): structured Alabama bills, votes and
  legislators.
- **Governor's office** (governor.alabama.gov): executive orders, releases.
- **Alabama Secretary of State** (sos.alabama.gov): elections, filings.
- State agencies: Medicaid, Education (ALSDE), Public Health (ADPH), Commerce.

## Alabama news (cover the spectrum and cross-check)
- AL.com · Alabama Reflector (nonprofit) · Alabama Daily News ·
  Alabama Political Reporter · Yellowhammer News (right-leaning) ·
  1819 News (right-leaning) · local TV (WSFA, WBRC, WAFF, WKRG) and regional
  papers.
- Use the right-leaning outlets to understand how the audience is hearing a
  story, and to quote their framing fairly.

## Data
- U.S. Census (ACS) and BLS (unemployment, wages by metro).
- KFF (health coverage), USDA ERS (rural and farm), Alabama Department of
  Revenue.

## Community, events, jobs
- Alabama Tourism (alabama.travel) events calendar; city and county sites;
  university calendars.
- **AlabamaWorks** (alabamaworks.com) and Alabama JobLink (joblink.alabama.gov).
- Alabama Department of Commerce / "Made in Alabama": new plants and
  expansions with job counts.
- Alabama Community College System training programs; grants.gov and ADECA
  for grants.
