## The whole system in one page

1. **Pick the who first.** Go where proven buyers already shop: the "shoe
   stores". Sell to "shoe buyers" who are spending money now. 60% of the result
   is the list, 30% the offer and 10% the copy. [Z6, Z7, Z9, Z29]
2. **Find the symptom by listening, not guessing.** Use Reddit, Quora, Amazon
   reviews, community questions, interviews and past sales calls. Score each
   symptom on frequency and emotion. [Z8, Z13, Z23, Z26]
3. **Build the offer.** Fill the four quadrants: symptoms, outcome, new and
   exciting way out, and TIMER. The first two come from the market. Make it
   specific, often an add-on, and as close to the sale as possible. [Z9, Z21, Z28]
4. **Test with a handraiser.** Write 1–3 sentences in front of buyers. Nobody
   raises a hand → change one variable, usually the symptom. [Z1, Z7, Z14]
5. **Have the conversation.** Usually it's in text. Ask about their situation,
   parrot back, dig to street level, then ask "what else have you tried?" [Z4, Z9, Z13]
6. **Present the plan in 3–5 steps, one bite at a time.** "What do you think of
   that plan?" Objections happen at the plan, not at the price. [Z5, Z8, Z9]
7. **Close.** Ask "Can I tell you a little about what working together on that
   would look like?" Then IVQ pricing and a spelled-out next step. Send a
   5-minute Loom or Google Doc when the explanation needs it. [Z4, Z6, Z8, Z12]
8. **Follow up by going deeper on the plan,** never with "did you see my
   proposal?" A no usually means "not now". Keep watering the garden. [Z14, Z18, Z29]
9. **Scale through shoe stores,** not volume. Give first to the people who
   already hold the buyers. Be a giver of cool ideas: the ideas are free and you
   charge for implementation. [Z6, Z27, Z30]

## Core beliefs

- "We're really building real relationships at scale." He aims for "forever
  money", not a "six-month hero run" built on a script that gets copied. [Z29, Z30]
- "I want the offer to do the selling, not me." [Z6, Z7]
- "In a high ticket sale, they're buying their way out of their problem a lot
  more than they're buying their way into your solution." [Z8]
- "If you can describe the pain better than they can, they assume you have the
  solution." [Z25]
- "If they agree with the plan, they will buy it." [Z4]
- "Great copywriting is not guessing, it's parroting." [Z10, Z14]
- "Specificity prints cash." [Z4, Z6, Z22]
- "The thing that kills sales more than anything else is neediness." [Z9]
- "Don't automate what doesn't work." Write 10 by hand first. [Z4, Z5, Z15]
- "Money is a delayed effect from inputs." Commit to inputs for at least 90 days. [Z5, Z29]
- Never talk about money without the value exchanged. The invoice is a give, not a take. [Z29]
- Avoid grandiose promises. Promise the most small-but-meaningful ONE of
  something. [Z7, Z14, Z22]
- Prefer pull to push. Make them believe they found you. Act as the "heart
  surgeon", not a door knocker. [Z9, Z29]
- Mentors and influences he names:
  - Travis Sago, who started the phoneless, pre-selling approach [Z6, Z24];
  - Jay Abraham [Z8, Z23, Z30];
  - Dan Kennedy [Z28, Z29];
  - *The Go-Giver* [Z27, Z30].
