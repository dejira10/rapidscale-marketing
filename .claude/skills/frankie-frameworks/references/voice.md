The rulebook for drafts is `style-guide.md` (G1). This file holds the

## How he sounds

- **Plain, casual and conversational**: "Hey dude", "my man", "Is it cool if I
  share?", "No rush". Write the way you talk. [Z4, Z5, Z30]
- **Short messages**, with one idea per message and clear beating clever.
  Simple, third-grade English. [Z5, Z14]
- **Humour that lowers pressure.** Examples: the follow-up about being kidnapped
  by aliens, playful sign-offs ("may the force be with you"), GIFs in Messenger. [Z8, Z25]
- **Honest about limits.** "Honestly, I don't know what will happen until we run
  this." "I wish I could tell you it worked like that, but that's just not my
  experience." [Z7, Z14]
- **About them, not him.** He counts the I's and uses their words back to them
  (parroting). [Z4, Z10]
- **Calm and specific** in client-facing Looms. The auto-detailing Loom has no
  hype and no profanity. [Z2]
- **Analogies he reuses:**
  - shoe stores and shoe buyers;
  - the golf course;
  - the hammer and the plane ride;
  - Heaven Island, Hell Island and the Sharks;
  - the garden;
  - coffee date, first date, marriage;
  - nickels vs $100 bills;
  - the barbecue invite. [throughout]

## Rules for this repo

- **No profanity.** Frankie swears a lot in coaching calls, but the user asked
  for none. Rewrite quotes cleanly and keep the meaning.
- **Leave out these, even though they appear in the sources:**
  - the racial-caricature skit;
  - crude jokes;
  - deceptive "naughty tricks";
  - comments about specific people's appearance;
  - personal stories that don't bear on the method.
- **Don't invent** Frankie quotes, client results or numbers. Numbers in these
  files are examples from his material. Never present them as results for a new
  client.
