  It's "the most boring message you can send." [S01, S04]

  ask for your permit number." "Same building, smaller bill." [S05, S10, S16]

  tone [S08, S09]. "Quiet authority." [S18, S50]

  S03, S11].

  bounty hunters, holding a door, pizza slices), philosophy quotes (Jung,
  Munger, Schopenhauer), and post-mortems on why something worked [S04, S05,
  S13, S17, S37].

Apply his principles in the user's own voice. Don't write as Saad, sign as
Saad, or claim his results.

## Honesty flags (consolidated)

Never produce these, even though they appear in his material:

1. **Fabricated proof**:
   - "a friend of mine told me" [S24];
   - "I know someone who helped a new {industry} CMO…" [S58];
   - "I've vetted 50+ agencies" or "I've connected 20 companies, even if you
     didn't" [S39];
   - "{company} came up in that context" [S31];
   - "social proof out of thin air" [S31], unless the conversations really
     happened;
   - other people's "white-label" case studies passed off as your own [S17].
2. **Claiming experience you don't have**:
   - "Yes, I have worked with cannabis before" [S41];
   - "I spend a lot of time around K-12 technology" after one hour [S09];
   - "basically what I do is…" on day one [S12].

   Say what's true, e.g. "I track this market and route live situations."
3. **Misleading terms**: "performance basis" while charging upfront [S21].
4. **Hiding that both sides pay** [S39]. Use the S08 transparency line.
5. **Fake signals of hand-sending**: "Sent from my iPhone" and deliberate typos
   [S19, S39, S60].
6. **Scarcity or demand claims that aren't true**:
   - "I have a few other teams I can route this to" [S17];
   - "two more situations came in this week" [S33];
   - "I'm speaking with multiple plants" [S39].

   Use them only when literally true.
7. **Privacy-sensitive targeting**:
   - liquidity events and post-exit wealth [S23];
   - personal emails and mobile numbers [S46].

   If used at all, use only public business information, and go "market level"
   rather than quoting their exit amount (Saad's own advice in S23).
8. **Harm-based lanes** (OSHA fatalities): factual, respectful copy only [S50].
9. **Cannabis** is federally illegal in the US. Use it only where it's
   state-legal, and flag it first [S15].
10. **Pressure and status tactics** from the role-play [S23]:
    - refusing a written proposal;
    - "doubling down" when called out.

    Use the honest parts instead: a clear delivery standard, a written summary
    on request, and respect for their legal review.
