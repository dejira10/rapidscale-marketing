# Saad — Source Index

Every `[Sn]` tag in these reference files points to a row here. All 61 sources
are PDF transcripts of Saad Belcaid's videos, uploaded by the user as two zips:

- `Saad_Belcaid_Connector_1.zip`: S01–S17, his current "connector" videos;
- `Saad_Belcaid_YouTube.zip`: S18–S61, YouTube videos from both of his eras.

The transcripts are machine transcriptions without timestamps, so tags cite the
source only. Names, numbers and tool names are sometimes garbled ("OSGA" means
OSHA, "Epify" means Apify, "ConnectOS" also appears as "Connectro" or
"ConnectorOS"). The references correct obvious garbles and flag the rest.

**Era** matters:

- **Connector**: his current model. He sells *access* between two sides of a
  market and charges for qualified introductions or retainers. This is the
  doctrine these skills follow.
- **Agency (earlier)**: his earlier "AI sales systems agency" model. He built
  Make.com and n8n lead-gen systems and sold them or the meetings they booked.
  He later rejects selling AI, automation and meetings [S01, S03, S31]. Use
  this era for tooling, signals and personalization technique, never as the
  offer.
