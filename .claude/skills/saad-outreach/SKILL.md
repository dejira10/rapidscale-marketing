# Saad — Outreach

Use only Saad's material here. Outreach for Frankie and Aaron differs.

## Inputs

1. From `saad-frameworks`, read:
   - `references/outreach.md` (copy, follow-ups, replies);
   - `references/closing-and-delivery.md` (closes, intros, after);
   - `references/offers-and-pricing.md` (offer wording, guarantee, language
     discipline, transparency line);
   - `references/user-guide.md` (**the rulebook**: quiet authority, outreach
     structure, qualification);
   - `references/style-guide.md` (Saad's tone in detail);
   - `references/voice-and-exclusions.md` (honesty flags and exclusions).
2. A lane brief from `saad-market-research`, or verified facts about the
   specific prospects on both sides.

If you don't know both sides and the trigger, run `saad-market-research` first.

## Pick the right piece

| Situation | Use | Where |
|---|---|---|
| First email, supply side | "I have {demand} actively {doing X} right now. Before I route anyone anywhere, wanted to understand your capacity…" | `outreach.md` §1–2 |
| First email, demand side | "I have {suppliers} who {solve X}. Want me to put one or two in front of you?" or "checking the issue is still active" | `outreach.md` §1–2 |
| Trigger-based opener | Lead with the dated public fact (license date, permit, lost bid, warning letter) | `outreach.md` §2 |
| Follow-ups | 3–4 steps: capacity check, door-open line, breakup. After a ghost, send evidence, not "checking in" | `outreach.md` §3 |
| They replied | Watermelon rule → mirror their words, hand nothing over, two times. Answer price questions directly | `outreach.md` §5 |
| Skeptic or "is this lead gen?" | Selective referral/introduction model reply; pull away calmly | `outreach.md` §5 table |
| Both sides confirmed | Close email from your own Gmail: fee, invoice, what they get, replacement clause, transparency line if both pay | `closing-and-delivery.md` §3 |
| The free side | Warm note: "no fee or obligation on your side," the repeat-source line, the case-study video ask | `closing-and-delivery.md` §3, §6 |
| Paid | Intro email ("Intro — quick context," or full context) and optionally a thesis brief | `closing-and-delivery.md` §4–5 |
| After the intro | ~24-hour check-in, classify, repeat-sale line, continuation or retainer | `closing-and-delivery.md` §6, `offers-and-pricing.md` §6 |
| Supply call | Quiet-authority opener, 80% intel, 20% transition to retainer | `closing-and-delivery.md` §2 |

## Steps

1. Pick the piece and cite the source tags you're modeling.
2. Draft it in plain language:
   - short;
   - the industry's own words;
   - one clear CTA that can be answered yes or no;
   - text-only;
   - no flattery or AI-sounding personalization.

   The personalization is the specific public fact.
3. **Apply `user-guide.md`, then `style-guide.md`,** and run both final checks.
   Default to asking interest or permission before asking for a meeting
   (`user-guide-review.md`).
4. **Run the honesty check** (`voice-and-exclusions.md`). Every claim must be
   true for the user, including:
   - "I have…";
   - "multiple";
   - "came through this week";
   - "I've worked with…";
   - scarcity and social proof.

   Rewrite or remove any you can't confirm, and list what needs confirming.
5. **Never name the other party before payment.** Keep "language discipline":
   no "my clients" or "we represent."
6. If both sides pay, include the transparency line [S08].
7. Output:
   - the draft(s);
   - the subject line(s);
   - the sequence timing;
   - the source tags;
   - the facts still to verify;
   - the sending settings (`stack.md`).

## Compliance

- **Email:** follow CAN-SPAM. Use a real sender identity and a physical address,
  and include a working opt-out ("one word back and I'll stop" [S02] doesn't
  replace a compliant opt-out).
- **SMS and calls:** TCPA applies. Don't text or auto-call without consent.
- **Personal contact data:** don't use personal emails or mobile numbers for
  cold outreach.
- **Harm-sensitive lanes** (fatalities, enforcement): factual and respectful
  only.
